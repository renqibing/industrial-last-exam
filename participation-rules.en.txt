Difficulty and paper co-authorship

We test GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 against the same acceptance criteria and average their scores (out of 1). You do not need to test the task yourself.
Near-term: average score below 0.5 (50%). At least 3 accepted tasks qualify you for paper co-authorship.
Last-exam: average score below 0.1 (10%). At least 1 accepted task qualifies you for paper co-authorship.
Meeting either threshold qualifies you for paper co-authorship as a Data Contributor. Each additional variant of the same task counts as 1/3 of a task. Reference results must come from actual runs, with clear acceptance criteria.
Before release, we will retest and select tasks using the latest strong models so the benchmark remains challenging when published.
Outstanding contributions may receive cash awards, under the reward rules announced by this project.
